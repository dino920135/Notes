- #3DPrinting #Filament [[Comparison of 3D printing filaments]]
- What to set on the printer / slicer (P2S + BambuStudio)
- | Filament | Nozzle °C | Bed °C | Enclosure | Dry before use |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | PLA | 190–220 | 50–60 | No (door open) | 50–55 °C, 8 h |
  | PETG | 220–250 | 70–85 | No | 65 °C, 8 h |
  | ABS | 230–260 | 90–110 | Yes | 80 °C, 8 h |
  | ASA | 240–260 | 90–110 | Yes | 80 °C, 8 h |
  | TPU | 210–230 | 30–60 | No | 70 °C, 8 h |
  | Nylon | 250–280 | 70–100 | Usually | 80 °C, 12 h |
  | PC | 260–300+ | 100–120 | Yes | 80 °C, 8 h |
- PLA on an enclosed printer: open the door or remove the top lid, otherwise the chamber gets too warm and can cause clogs
- Values are general guidelines, check the spool label for the exact range
